These are the podcasts that shape how I think, build, and lead. They range from tactical to philosophical. 

## AI

- *AI&I* (Dan Shipper) - specificity over speculation, from people who use AI daily
- *How I AI* (Claire Vo) - creator of ChatPRD interviews other builders
- *Latent Space* (Alessio and swyx) - great mix of interviews and technical deep-dives

## Business

- *Acquired* (Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal) - business storytelling at its best
- *Invest Like the Best* (Patrick O'Shaughnessy) - consistently insightful interview with leading investors
- *Lenny's Podcast* (Lenny Ratchisky) - behind-the-scenes at leading tech companies
- *Odd Lots* (Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway) - fun, in an economic nerd way

## Data

- *Data Engineering Podcast* (Tobias Macey) - formative during my years as an data science/engineering IC
- *The Engineering Leadership Podcast* (Patrick Gallagher and Jerry Li) - more relevant in my transition to IC

## Hard to Classify

- *Conversations with Tyler* (Tyler Cowen) - outlier for intellectual curiosity and cultural range
- *Dialectic* (Jackson Dahl)- unqiue interviews with builders and thinkers
- *Econtalk* (Russ Roberts) - expand my worldview through an economic lens
